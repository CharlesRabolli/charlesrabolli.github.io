I'm a physician-scientist in training at The Ohio State University, finishing my MD in the NIH-funded Medical Scientist Training Program. My PhD in Biomedical Engineering asked how a single chemical mark on messenger RNA, m<sup>6</sup>A, shapes the way the heart responds to stress and how the heart uses it to signal to the rest of the body. That work was supported by an NIH F30 fellowship and led to first-author papers in *Circulation*, *JCI Insight*, and *RNA*.

These days I'm focused on pathology and on how machine learning is changing it: I'm training clinically in pathology and have annotated whole-slide images for computational pathology research. I trained in the U.S., in Italy as a Fulbright Scholar, and in Germany. I'm also active in biotech entrepreneurship through Nucleate.
