DRACH means D = A/G/U, R = A/G, then the methylated A, then C, then H = A/C/U. In random sequence of equal base composition about 1 position in 57 matches, so a 2 kb mRNA carries dozens of candidate sites. Only a small fraction of them are methylated in cells. The motif is necessary but nowhere near sufficient; position on the transcript, exon structure, and the writer complex's access decide the rest.
