Pixels = tissue size ÷ microns per pixel. Tiles assume about 70% of the bounding box is tissue after background removal. Scanned files are compressed (often roughly 1–3 GB at 40×), but models still have to process every tile. The heatmap is illustrative: it shows how attention-based models (Part V) concentrate on a small fraction of tiles.
